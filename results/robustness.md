| Main (IMO 2015-2024, population >= 1M) | 157 | 32% | 88% | 8.3% | 0.2% | +8.6 |
| Took part in at least 5 of 10 IMOs | 157 | 22% | 80% | 8.3% | 0.2% | +7.9 |
| All countries, any population | 212 | 42% | 63% | 13.7% | 2.4% | +8.3 |
| Earlier decade (IMO 2005-2014) | 158 | 30% | 80% | 6.7% | 0.7% | +6.1 |

In every specification the poorest quarter of countries is the least likely to send a team, wins a far smaller share of gold medals than its share of contestants, and richer countries score higher. The size of the gaps depends on the choices: including micro-states, for example, raises the poorest group's gold share from 0.2% to 2.4%.
